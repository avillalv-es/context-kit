---
description: Record a finding, or aggregate the findings log into LESSONS.md
---

Use the `finding-ledger` skill.

With no argument, or with a description of something you just settled: record
it as an entry.

With `reflect`: aggregate every entry into `findings/LESSONS.md` and report
the summary line.

With `read` or `lessons`: read `findings/LESSONS.md` and summarize where the
investigation currently stands.

What to record, or the mode: $ARGUMENTS
